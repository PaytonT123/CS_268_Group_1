Who is Dana...

Hello! I am Dana Triebold, founder of DKT Empowerment Solutions. I believe every woman can rediscover her light and get unstuck, no matter the season of life. I am here to walk alongside you as you breathe life into your new identity and embrace a wellness journey rooted in warmth and self-compassion.

I know how it feels to be at a crossroads. From navigating health challenges to raising a family and starting anew, my own pivots taught me how to redefine my identity. Today, I use those lessons to help you move through your transitions with gentle resilience, finding clarity as you grow.
