Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? You might find the answer here.

What is life coaching?

Life coaching is a partnership that helps you get clear on what you want, figure out what is holding you back, and take steps toward the life you want to live. It focuses on where you are now and where you want to go.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is not a replacement for therapy or medical care. Coaching focuses on goals, growth, and moving forward. If you are dealing with a mental health concern, please reach out to a licensed therapist or doctor.

How does the free discovery call work?

It is a relaxed 20-minute call by phone or video. We will get to know each other and talk about whether coaching is a good fit for you. There is no pressure and no commitment.

Are sessions in person or virtual?

Both! You can meet in person at Pathway Wellness in Delafield, or virtually from wherever you are.

How much does coaching cost?

A single 60-minute session is $100. The coaching package includes 6 sessions plus email check-ins for $450. See the Services & Pricing page for more details.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a session.
